My North

They say, and one should always be cautious of what “They” say, that a sensible person should only rite about what they genuinely know. Boring, right? I, for one, prefer to write about what fascinates me, which is a completely different and infinitely more entertaining matter. Knowledge is static; fascination is a wild, ongoing party.

This journal, my personal theatre, is my way of refusing to be boxed in. The world loves neat labels, but people? We’re gloriously messy, full of contradictions, quirks, and occasional flashes of brilliance. My writing will reflect that complexity, skipping effortlessly from the sublime to the strategic.

We’ll stare into the Cosmos. That vast, mind boggling canvas of physics and space. Here, we’ll marvel at the absurdity of human egos trying to measure the universe. Black holes, dark matter sure, those are fascinating but maybe the most interesting light is the one we shine ourselves.

We’ll peek into History. Forget dry dates and dusty battles; the past is the ultimate source of juicy gossip. We’ll unearth forgotten scandals, laugh at repeated mistakes, and maybe just maybe learn something about ourselves along the way.

We’ll examine Society. With the precision of a surgeon and the detachment of a diplomat, we’ll navigate social issues, trends, and human theatrics. Outrage will be optional; sharp observation mandatory. Wit will be our weapon, verbosity our enemy.

And we’ll celebrate Chess. That elegant, 64-square universe of strategy where true genius isn’t about brute strength, but foresight. We’ll explore gambits, endgames, and the quiet poetry of a well placed knight. It’s philosophy disguised as a game and, yes, demolishing your opponent with style is encouraged.

In short, I promise you wit, wonder, and a warranted impertinence. Nothing less would be worth the sheer, exhausting effort of putting pen to digital paper.
